## 4. Advertising and User Choices

Our Game displays third-party advertisements via Google AdMob to keep the Game free to play.

### Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Ads
Depending on your region and your device settings, ads may be personalized (tailored to your interests based on your advertising identifier) or non-personalized (contextual, based only on the game content).

### How to Opt Out of Interest-Based Advertising
You have complete control over interest-based advertising on your Android device:
1. Open your device's **Settings**.
2. Navigate to **Google** > **All Services** (or **Privacy**).
3. Select **Ads**.
4. Tap **Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**, or toggle on **Opt out of Ads Personalization**.

Once deleted or opted out, ad networks cannot track your activity across apps for behavioral profiling, though you will still see contextual ads.

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at allgames9e@gmail.com. We will respond to your verified request within the timeframe required by applicable law.
